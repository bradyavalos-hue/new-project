About

Homes first. Yield follows.

I acquire, renovate, and operate small rental properties with a long horizon. The work is practical: sound structures, clean interiors, reliable tenants, and books that stay honest.

I look for buildings with good bones in neighborhoods people actually want to live in. Then I keep them that way — repairs done early, communication kept short and clear, and no surprises on either side of the lease.

  • Focus 1–4 unit rentals
  • Approach Buy, improve, hold
  • Standard Live-in quality, not flip quality
